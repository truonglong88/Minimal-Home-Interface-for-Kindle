# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. This is a personal project whose source is published
as-is.

- **Bug reports, ideas and questions:** post them in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/truonglong88/Minimal-Home-Interface-for-Kindle/discussions). They are read, but there is no promise of a fix, a reply
  or a new feature.
- **Pull requests are not accepted** and are closed automatically. Issues are
  turned off.
- **Forks are welcome:** change the project however you like under the terms of
  the [AGPL-3.0 license](LICENSE) (in short: if you distribute your version,
  share its source under the same license). The images are not covered by the
  license: see [docs/images/NOTICE.md](docs/images/NOTICE.md).
