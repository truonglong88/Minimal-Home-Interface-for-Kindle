# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. This is a personal project whose source is published
as-is: **pull requests and issues are not accepted or reviewed**, and pull
requests are closed automatically.

You are welcome to **fork** the project and change it however you like, under
the terms of the [AGPL-3.0 license](LICENSE) (in short: if you distribute your
version, share its source under the same license). Note that the images are not
covered by the license: see [docs/images/NOTICE.md](docs/images/NOTICE.md).
